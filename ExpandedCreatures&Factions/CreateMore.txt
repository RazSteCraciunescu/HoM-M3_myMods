Creating additional creatures
A standard-library-only generator is included. From Command Prompt in the extracted package:


py -3 tools\new_creature_mod.py --id shadowStag --name "Shadow Stag" --plural "Shadow Stags" --base unicorn --faction neutral --level 5



War Unicorn-derived creature:

py -3 tools\new_creature_mod.py --id blackCharger --name "Black Charger" --base war-unicorn --faction inferno --level 6



Ballista-derived machine:

py -3 tools\new_creature_mod.py --id boltEngine --name "Bolt Engine" --base ballista --faction neutral --level 5



It creates a complete new mod folder with:

mod.json;
creature configuration;
placeholder icons;
placeholder adventure-map animation;
base animation and sound references.
